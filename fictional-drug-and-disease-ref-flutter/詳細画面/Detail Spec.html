W1 + W2 per-value chip color SSOT

v1.4 · alpha-tint formula · WCAG AA ≥ 4.5:1 both modes

Formula (both W1 and W2): light bg = base @ 5% alpha-blended over #FFFFFF ; dark bg = base @ 20% alpha-blended over #181B20 . Foreground = base hex with iterative HSL-lightness adjustment (step 0.04, max 16 steps; light → darker, dark → lighter) until contrast on the rendered background reaches ≥ 4.5:1. Host cell wrapper ( .eg-cell-l / .eg-cell-d ) is unchanged from v1.3.

W1 (4 enums, 33 values): base hexes are EXACT verbatim copies from lib/ui/search/constants/search_palette.dart . Not designed here — mirrored from the running Flutter search screen. W2 (6 enums, 61 values): designed here, no two values within an enum share a hex.
